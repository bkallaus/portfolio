DUEL

A two-player strategy card game for the browser. Three ages, two cities, and three ways to win — built for two people to play together remotely.

React TypeScript WebRTC Vite
Play the game →

How to play

Same screen

Pass the laptop back and forth, or sit side by side. Nothing to set up.

Two devices

The game connects the two browsers directly to each other, peer to peer, with no account and no server in between.
